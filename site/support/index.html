Support

Current iOS 1.0 app: local security and QR-content reading
Current app scope: OrchidPay provides local session protection, device-security checks, a security-event log and QR-content inspection. It does not provide banking accounts, balances, cards, transfers, deposits or payments. The QR reader does not validate recipients or initiate transactions.

General support

Email contact@purpleorchidgroup.com with the subject OrchidPay support. Mention your device platform, app version/build, the screen concerned and the steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords, card data or unnecessary personal information.

For App Review

The currently submitted iOS 1.0 build 12 exposes Home, Verify QR, Activity and Profile. From Home or Profile, open Security to inspect device protection and the biometric test. Verify QR displays locally scanned QR content without opening links or initiating a payment. Activity shows locally recorded security/session events. Profile contains security, help and privacy information. A banking account, a payment service login or a financial test account is not required for these screens.

We welcome requests for clarification on any screen or unexpected behavior. Please communicate through App Store Connect's App Review messaging channel, or contact support using the email above. This page does not imply that App Review has approved the app.

Security reports

For a suspected vulnerability, email contact@purpleorchidgroup.com with the subject [SECURITY] OrchidPay. Describe reproducible steps and impact without accessing anyone else's data or exploiting the issue beyond what is necessary.

Assistance OrchidPay

La version iOS 1.0 actuellement présentée comprend la protection locale de session, les contrôles de sécurité de l'appareil, le journal local et la lecture de contenu QR.
