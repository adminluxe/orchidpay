Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026
Current status. OrchidPay is a private pilot initiative of Purple Orchid Group. The current iOS pilot does not enable live payments, customer-fund custody or production settlement. This policy covers the OrchidPay public website and the current pilot application.

1. Who is responsible for the data?

Until the planned Luxembourg entity is formally incorporated and assumes the relevant processing activities, the transitional controller is Raoul FANKEM, founder of the Purple Orchid Group project, acting in his own name for this initiative.

Public correspondence location: 1160 Auderghem, Brussels, Belgium.
Project office: 14 Rue de la Fonderie, L-1581 Luxembourg.
Privacy contact: contact@purpleorchidgroup.com.

2. Data processed by the public website

  • Information you voluntarily send by email or another support channel, including your name, contact details, message and attachments you choose to provide.
  • Limited technical, security and network information that may be processed by hosting, DNS, TLS, anti-abuse or content-delivery providers in order to deliver and protect the website.
  • The current website source deliberately includes no advertising pixels, behavioural analytics or social-media tracking scripts.

3. Data processed by the current iOS pilot

The current pilot is designed around local demonstration data and mock flows. OrchidPay does not intentionally collect production payment credentials, real card PAN/CVV data, real bank credentials, real transaction settlement data, advertising identifiers, contact books, precise location, health data or cross-app tracking data through its own production backend in this pilot.

The app may rely on Apple operating-system services and distribution/update infrastructure. Those technical services can process device, network or diagnostic information under their own terms and technical requirements. When Face ID is enabled in a future build, biometric templates remain managed by iOS; OrchidPay is not designed to receive the underlying biometric template.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Support and correspondence: responding to requests and taking steps requested by you.
  • Security and service delivery: protecting the website, diagnosing faults and preventing abuse on the basis of legitimate interests in operating a secure pilot.
  • Compliance: retaining or disclosing information where required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Consent: where future optional processing legally requires consent, it will remain disabled until valid consent is obtained.

5. Recipients and transfers

Necessary infrastructure or communications providers may process limited information to deliver hosting, DNS, network protection, email, app distribution or update services. OrchidPay does not sell personal data and the current landing does not include advertising or behavioural-tracking partners. If providers process data outside the EEA, applicable transfer mechanisms and safeguards are used where required by law.

6. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, to maintain security, to manage an ongoing relationship, or to comply with applicable legal obligations and claims. The pilot does not currently maintain a production customer ledger or live payment history.

7. Your rights

Subject to the General Data Protection Regulation and applicable law, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You can submit a request through Privacy Choices. You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (APD/GBA) or another competent EEA supervisory authority.

8. Children

The OrchidPay pilot is not designed as a child-directed service. The current product should not be interpreted as a public financial account for minors.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated before live payment features, production accounts, regulated payment services, material analytics or materially different data processing are introduced.
