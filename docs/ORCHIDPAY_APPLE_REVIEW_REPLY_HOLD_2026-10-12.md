## Réponse de réserve (uniquement si Apple demande une précision avant la décision)

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for continuing the review of OrchidPay iOS 1.0.0 (build 12).

For clarity, the customer-facing build provides local app-session security information, an on-device security-event log and a QR-code content reader. The QR screen only displays a local text preview; it does not verify a payment recipient, launch a transfer or execute a financial transaction. Account balances, bank accounts, payment cards and money transfers are not available through the current navigation.

We identified older App Store and website descriptions that suggested financial features which this build does not offer. We have prepared corrected information and are checking what can be updated in the current review state without interrupting your review. We will not resubmit or remotely unlock undisclosed financial functionality.

If your team was able to access a financial screen, could you please share the navigation steps or a screenshot so we can investigate the exact submitted binary?

Kind regards,
The OrchidPay Team
